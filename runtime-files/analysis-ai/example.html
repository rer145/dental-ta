Input Tooth Scores

Enter tooth formation scores (2-17) or leave blank for missing teeth:

Score meanings: 2=CI (Cusp initiation), 3=COC (Coalescence of cusps), 4=CUO (Cusp outline complete), 5=I1/2 (Crown 1/2 complete), 6=CRC (Crown 3/4 complete), 7=RI (Root initiation), 8=CLI (Cleft initiation), 9=RC (Root 1/4), 10=R1/2, 11=R3/4, 12=RC (Root complete), 13=A1/2, 14=AC, 15=EWJ, 16=CE
